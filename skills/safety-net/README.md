# Safety net

A Claude skill for people who are new to AI. It keeps them from sharing passwords, card details and ID, asks before connecting anything, never touches money without fresh permission, and flags scams.

## Install

- **Claude app or claude.ai:** download this folder as a zip, then upload it as a skill in your Claude settings.
- **Claude Code:** copy this folder to `~/.claude/skills/safety-net/`.
- **ChatGPT, Gemini or anything else:** copy the short version from [myaicraft.uk/start-here.html](https://myaicraft.uk/start-here.html) into your custom instructions.
