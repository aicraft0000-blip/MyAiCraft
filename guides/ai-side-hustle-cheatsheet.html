The AI Side Hustle Cheatsheet

10 Proven Ways to Earn Money Using AI Tools Right Now

You don't need a computer science degree. You don't need £10,000 in savings. You need a laptop, an internet connection, and the willingness to start before you feel ready. This cheatsheet gives you 10 specific ways to earn money with AI tools today. Not "someday." Today.

How to Use This Guide

Each method includes five things:

  1. What it is — the service you'll offer
  2. Tools needed — free or low-cost AI tools to do the work
  3. Expected earnings — realistic monthly income once established
  4. Time investment — hours per week to get going
  5. First step today — one action you can take in the next 60 minutes

No fluff. No theory. Pick one method, do the first step, and build from there.

Method 1: AI-Assisted Copywriting

What it is: Writing website copy, email sequences, and sales pages for businesses using AI as your drafting partner. You bring the strategy and editing skill; AI handles the first draft.

Tools needed: ChatGPT Plus (£16/month) or Claude Pro (£16/month). Grammarly free tier for polishing. Google Docs for delivery.

Expected earnings: £800–£2,500/month with 3–5 regular clients. A single sales page runs £200–£500. Email sequences of 5 emails go for £300–£600.

Time investment: 10–15 hours per week. Most of that is client communication and editing, not writing from scratch.

First step today: Go to Upwork, create a profile, and search for "copywriter" or "email copywriter" jobs posted in the last 24 hours. Send 3 proposals. Use AI to write a sample email sequence for a fictional coffee shop to attach as a portfolio piece.

Method 2: AI Image Generation for Print-on-Demand

What it is: Creating designs for t-shirts, mugs, posters, and phone cases using AI image generators, then selling them through print-on-demand platforms. No inventory, no shipping.

Tools needed: Midjourney (£8/month) or Leonardo AI (free tier available). Canva free for layout. Redbubble, Merch by Amazon, or Printful for fulfillment.

Expected earnings: £200–£1,200/month depending on volume. Each design takes 15–30 minutes. Top sellers create 50–100 designs per month. Individual designs earn £1–£5/month passively, and winners can hit £20–£50/month each.

Time investment: 8–12 hours per week for design creation and upload.

First step today: Sign up for Leonardo AI (free) and Redbubble (free). Generate 5 designs around a niche you know well — dog breeds, hiking quotes, or programming humour. Upload all 5 with keyword-rich titles before you close your laptop.

Method 3: AI-Powered Social Media Management

What it is: Managing social media accounts for local businesses. You use AI to generate content ideas, write captions, and create image concepts. Most small businesses know they need social media but hate doing it.

Tools needed: ChatGPT or Claude for caption writing. Canva for graphics. Buffer or Later free tier for scheduling.

Expected earnings: £300–£600 per client per month. Three clients puts you at £900–£1,800/month. Local businesses (restaurants, gyms, salons) are the easiest to land because they're underserved.

Time investment: 5–8 hours per week per client once you have a system.

First step today: Walk down your local high street and note 5 businesses with weak or inactive social media. Create a mock week of content (7 posts with captions and image descriptions) for one of them using AI. Email or visit the owner with: "I made this for your business — want me to handle your social media for £400/month?"

Method 4: AI Chatbot Setup for Small Businesses

What it is: Building simple AI chatbots for business websites using no-code tools. These bots answer common questions, book appointments, and capture leads while the business owner sleeps.

Tools needed: Tidio (free tier), Chatbase ($19/month), or Botpress (free tier). You'll feed the bot information from the business's website and FAQs.

Expected earnings: £300–£800 per chatbot setup, plus £50–£150/month for maintenance. Five clients on maintenance contracts gives you £250–£750/month recurring.

Time investment: 4–6 hours per chatbot build. 2–3 hours per month for maintenance across all clients.

First step today: Sign up for Chatbase's free tier. Build a demo chatbot using a local restaurant's menu and FAQ page (publicly available information). Record a 2-minute screen recording showing it working. Post it on LinkedIn with an offer to build one for any local business.

Method 5: AI-Enhanced Virtual Assistant Services

What it is: Offering virtual assistant services where AI handles the heavy lifting — email drafting, data entry, research, calendar management. You charge VA rates but complete work in half the time.

Tools needed: ChatGPT for drafting and research. Notion or Trello for task management. Calendly free tier for scheduling. Google Workspace.

Expected earnings: £600–£2,000/month. VAs charge £12–£25/hour. With AI, you complete 2 hours of work in 1 hour, effectively doubling your rate.

Time investment: 15–25 hours per week depending on client load.

First step today: Create a profile on Belay, Time Etc, or Fiverr under "Virtual Assistant." List specific services: email management, research, data entry, travel planning. Apply to 5 open VA positions today.

Method 6: AI Course and Tutorial Creation

What it is: Creating short online courses or tutorials teaching people how to use specific AI tools for their work. You don't need to be an expert — you need to be one step ahead of your students.

Tools needed: ChatGPT or Claude for content outlining. Loom (free) or OBS (free) for screen recording. Canva for slides. Gumroad, Udemy, or Skillshare for selling.

Expected earnings: £300–£3,000/month depending on topic and platform. A well-positioned Udemy course earns £200–£800/month passively. Gumroad courses with your own audience can hit £1,000+/month.

Time investment: 20–30 hours to create a course. 2–3 hours per week for marketing after launch.

First step today: Pick one AI tool you use well (ChatGPT, Midjourney, Canva AI). Write a 5-lesson outline teaching a specific outcome: "How to Write a Week of LinkedIn Posts in 30 Minutes Using ChatGPT." Record the first lesson as a free YouTube video to test interest.

Method 7: AI-Powered SEO Content Writing

What it is: Writing blog posts and website content for businesses that want to rank on Google. You use AI to research keywords, create outlines, and draft articles, then you edit for quality and accuracy.

Tools needed: ChatGPT or Claude for drafting. Ubersuggest free tier or Google Keyword Planner for keyword research. Surfer SEO ($49/month, worth it once you have 2+ clients) for content scoring.

Expected earnings: £500–£2,500/month. Blog posts run £50–£150 each. A client needing 8 posts per month pays £400–£1,200. Two to three clients gets you into solid income territory.

Time investment: 10–15 hours per week.

First step today: Use Ubersuggest's free tier to find 3 low-competition keywords in a niche you understand (fitness, cooking, personal finance). Write one 1,500-word article using AI as your drafting partner. Post it as a writing sample and send cold pitches to 5 businesses in that niche.

Method 8: AI Product Description Writing for E-commerce

What it is: Writing product descriptions for online stores. Most e-commerce sellers have terrible descriptions. You use AI to create compelling, benefit-focused copy that sells.

Tools needed: ChatGPT or Claude for writing. Amazon and competitor research for market understanding.

Expected earnings: £400–£1,500/month. Product descriptions run £5–£20 each. A Shopify store with 100 products is a £500–£2,000 project. Amazon sellers need descriptions for new products constantly.

Time investment: 8–12 hours per week.

First step today: Go to Fiverr, search "product description writer," and study the top-rated sellers' gig pages. Create your own gig. Then go to an Amazon category, find 5 products with weak descriptions, rewrite them as samples, and attach them to your gig.

Method 9: AI Newsletter Curation and Writing

What it is: Running a niche newsletter that curates and summarises industry news, trends, and tips using AI. Monetise through sponsorships, affiliate links, and premium tiers.

Tools needed: ChatGPT or Claude for writing and summarising. Beehiiv (free up to 2,500 subscribers) or Substack (free). Feedly for source gathering.

Expected earnings: £0 for the first 2–3 months while building subscribers. £200–£2,000/month once you hit 1,000+ subscribers through sponsorships and affiliate revenue. Top niche newsletters with 5,000–10,000 subscribers earn £3,000–£8,000/month.

Time investment: 5–8 hours per week for a weekly newsletter.

First step today: Pick a niche with a clear audience: AI tools for real estate agents, marketing trends for small businesses, or tech news for non-technical founders. Sign up for Beehiiv. Write your first issue. Send it to 20 people you know in that space and ask them to subscribe and share.

Method 10: AI Presentation and Pitch Deck Design

What it is: Creating professional presentations and pitch decks for startups, consultants, and business owners. AI generates the structure and copy; you handle design and polish.

Tools needed: ChatGPT for content structure. Gamma AI (free tier) or Beautiful.ai ($12/month) for design. Canva Pro (£10/month) as backup.

Expected earnings: £500–£2,000/month. Pitch decks run £150–£500 each. Recurring clients (consultants who present weekly) pay £200–£400/month.

Time investment: 8–12 hours per week.

First step today: Go to Gamma AI and create a sample pitch deck for a fictional SaaS startup. Make it 10 slides, clean, and professional. Post the before/after on LinkedIn (show a bad PowerPoint vs. your AI-designed version). Add a line: "I build pitch decks for founders. DM me."

Your Action Plan

Don't try all 10. Pick one method — the one that matches your existing skills or interests.

This week: Complete the "first step today" for your chosen method. Set up your basic tools and profiles.

This month: Land your first paying client or make your first sale. Refine your process and pricing.

This quarter: Build to 3–5 regular clients or a consistent income stream. Create systems so you can handle more work in less time.

The people making money with AI aren't smarter than you. They started before they felt ready.
