How it works — from your niche to posted

  1. Tell it your niche. Skateboarding. Specialty coffee roasting. Family law. "MCP servers for fintech". As broad or as tight as your world actually runs — RadarWindow aims a scraper at its highest-volume sources and watches them around the clock.
  2. Three scores, not one. Every signal is rated on engagement, on recommendations, and on velocity — how fast it's being shared. You get the ones that top all three.
  3. Ahead before you've looked. A short signal digest lands daily — the top signals in your inbox and on your dashboard, ranked. Once a week, the one you pick becomes the full gated brief.
  4. Pick what's worth sharing. See one you want to post about? Flip a switch and RadarWindow writes it up — as briefs shaped for the exact platforms you post on.
  5. Three passes, no AI tells. Every brief runs a three-pass gate — generate, critique, revise — tuned to strip jargon and AI tells so it reads like you wrote it.
  6. One story, a post per account. You get one final brief plus a post derived from it for each account. Approve and post yourself — or connect a posting app like Blotato and let it publish for you.

The standard the gate enforces

Rejected (pass 2): "Unlock the transformative power of AI and take your content journey to the next level!"

Cleared to ship: "Anthropic cut input pricing 40% this week. What cost £8/M tokens now costs £4.80. Your eval budget just doubled itself."

Content with editorial oversight performs 4.1× better than raw automated output. The gate is that oversight — in code, on every brief, every week. The full methodology — sources, the scoring math, the three-pass gate, and what the pipeline refuses to do — is documented at radarwindow.com/methodology.
