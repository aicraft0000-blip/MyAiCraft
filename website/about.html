From the founder

I built from scratch, by need.
Then I built from the heart, by thirty-five years of wanting.

a letter

I'm 51. A couple of years ago, I could feel the ground shifting beneath me. AI was changing everything — I knew it, everyone kept saying it — but all I had were tools I barely understood and a creeping feeling I was already behind.

So I started from scratch. No tech background, no developer friends to call. Just me, a laptop, and a stubborn refusal to get left behind. I needed to and wanted to pivot, but to what? I started capturing everything I was doing and initially I thought if I was doing this for me, I could be doing this for you. But the truth is, AI is moving so quickly, that half way through the project, the technology has improved so much, what I was doing was obsolete.

But there was something else I needed. Something I'd been wanting for thirty-five years.

I threw myself into Quesi, a personal Astrologer on your mobile you can chat to whenever you want. Personally, I love her, she is wise, has points of view I've never considered and it's uniquely tailored to my Astrological chart — it's special.

If I could do this without a single line of code, so can you.

Astrology has been part of my life for as long as I can remember. I read everything. I listen to the podcasts. But here's the thing: unless you can afford a private reading, you're on your own. Staring at your natal chart, trying to remember what you have in which house at what degree, going back and forth between books and apps and notes, trying to piece together what it all means for you, right now.

It's complicated. It's time-consuming. And you never quite get the full picture.

Quesi was born from that frustration — and from something bigger than frustration. I wanted someone who already knew my chart. Who held all of it — the philosophy, the symbolism, the degrees — and could share it with me, warmly, whenever I needed it. She didn't exist. So I built her.

My second passion is being a truth seeker. I've always been drawn to metaphysics — to the philosophers and sages, to the ancient teachings that help us understand who we are and why we're here. However the one thing I've always run into is a human's bias, prejudice or lack of pure knowledge and wisdom, someone who can give me answers and explore and deepen my understanding and widen my perception of the spiritual world we live in and the rituals and faith we practise within it.

River was born from that need. Someone who you can talk to privately, ask any question, explore any number of religions or beliefs and through it all, feel safe, heard and understood. I hope you will experience the same feeling of soul sustenance I have.

That's what AiCraft is. Not just tools — a whole life, considered. The business side and the inner side. The practical and the personal. Both built from scratch, by someone who needs them both.

— Kay Santana Founder · AiCraft Holdings Ltd
