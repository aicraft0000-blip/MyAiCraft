At AiCraft, we believe in plain English — including here. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it when you visit myaicraft.uk or sign up to hear from us. We've written it to be read, not just stored. If anything is unclear, please get in touch.

01

Who we are

AiCraft is a community-first platform helping everyday people understand and use artificial intelligence — without jargon, overwhelm, or a computer science degree.

Data controller: AiCraft, operating at myaicraft.uk. For all data-related enquiries, contact us at hello@example.com.

We are based in England and this policy is written in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

02

What we collect

We only collect what we genuinely need. Here is what that looks like in practice:

  • Email address — when you join our waitlist or subscribe to our newsletter. That's it. We don't ask for your name, phone number, or anything else unless you choose to share it.
  • Questionnaire responses — if you complete our optional "help us build what you need" survey, we collect your answers (and optionally your email if you provide it). These are used solely to shape what we build.
  • Technical data — standard server logs including your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This is collected automatically and used only for security and performance monitoring. We do not use it to identify you personally.
  • Contact messages — if you email us directly, we retain the contents of that correspondence to respond to you.

We do not collect payment information on this site. Any future paid products will use a regulated third-party payment processor and their privacy policy will apply to that transaction.

03

How we use it

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send you our weekly newsletter and early access updates (if you've asked us to)
  • Respond to your questions and messages
  • Understand what our community wants so we can build it
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the website
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your data for automated decision-making, profiling, or anything that would affect you in a legally significant way.

04

Legal basis

Under UK GDPR, we must have a lawful basis for processing your data. Ours are:

  • Consent — when you sign up to our waitlist or newsletter, you are giving us clear, specific consent to send you communications. You can withdraw this at any time by clicking unsubscribe or emailing us.
  • Legitimate interests — for website security, fraud prevention, and understanding how our site performs. We've assessed that these interests do not override your rights.
  • Legal obligation — where we are required to retain data by law.

05

The Business Idea Tester

The Idea Tester on our homepage lets you type in a business idea and receive an AI-generated assessment instantly. We want to be completely transparent about how this works:

  • We do not store the ideas you submit. Your input is sent directly to the AI model to generate a response and is not saved to any AiCraft database or server.
  • Your ideas are not used to train AI models. We use the Anthropic API, and our usage does not contribute to model training.
  • No account is required. You can use the Idea Tester completely anonymously.
  • The tool processes your input via Anthropic's API. Anthropic's own privacy policy governs that processing — you can read it at anthropic.com/privacy.

In short: Your idea stays yours. We don't see it, store it, or share it. Type freely.

06

Sharing data

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with third parties for their marketing purposes. Full stop.

We may share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Email service providers — we use a third-party platform to send our newsletter (e.g. Mailchimp or similar). Your email address is shared with them solely to deliver communications you have requested. They act as data processors under our instruction.
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of AiCraft or others.
  • Business transfers — if AiCraft is ever acquired or merged, your data may transfer to the new owner, who will be bound by this policy or required to obtain fresh consent from you.

Any third parties we work with are required to handle your data in compliance with UK GDPR and may only use it for the purpose we specify.

07

How long we keep it

We keep your data only for as long as it is needed:

  • Newsletter / waitlist email addresses — kept for as long as you remain subscribed. When you unsubscribe, your email is removed within 30 days.
  • Questionnaire responses without email — stored anonymously and kept indefinitely as aggregate insight data.
  • Questionnaire responses with email — kept for up to 24 months, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Email correspondence — kept for up to 3 years for reference, then deleted.
  • Technical / server logs — typically retained for 30–90 days for security purposes.

08

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data. These are real rights — not small print.

Right of access

Ask us what personal data we hold about you and receive a copy of it.

Right to rectification

Ask us to correct any inaccurate or incomplete information.

Right to erasure

Ask us to delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to certain exceptions.

Right to restrict processing

Ask us to pause or limit how we use your data in certain circumstances.

Right to data portability

Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format to transfer elsewhere.

Right to withdraw consent

Unsubscribe or withdraw consent at any time. This won't affect anything we did before you withdrew.

Right to object

Object to processing based on legitimate interests. We will stop unless we have compelling grounds.

Right to complain

Lodge a complaint with the ICO (Information Commissioner's Office) if you believe we've mishandled your data.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@example.com. We will respond within 30 days. There is no charge for making a request.

09

Cookies

Our current website uses minimal cookies. At this stage we use only essential cookies required for the site to function — we do not use tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies that require your consent.

If this changes — for example, if we add Google Analytics or similar — we will update this policy, add a cookie banner, and ask for your consent before setting any non-essential cookies.

10

Children

AiCraft is intended for adults and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11

Changes to this policy

As AiCraft grows, this policy may be updated. When we make material changes, we will notify newsletter subscribers by email and update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We will never reduce your rights under this policy without giving you the opportunity to opt out first.
