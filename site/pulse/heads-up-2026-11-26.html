Black Friday: fake deals made with AI

Black Friday is tomorrow, and fake shops and holiday deals are easier to make convincing than ever. Check before you pay.

What's happening. On 20 November the UK Civil Aviation Authority warned that fake holiday deals are being made more convincing with AI, ahead of the Black Friday sales. City of London Police say fake websites and adverts are sometimes AI-enhanced, and that being asked to pay by bank transfer is a key warning sign. Barclays found that 76% of UK adults think AI has made scams more convincing, but only 34% feel confident spotting an AI-made shopping scam.

Who it affects. Anyone shopping or booking a holiday online this weekend, especially from an advert, a social media post, or a link a chatbot gave you.

What to do. Type the shop's address yourself instead of clicking a link. Pay by credit card if you can: for purchases between £100 and £30,000, Section 75 of the Consumer Credit Act means your card company may refund you if things go wrong. Never pay a shop or travel company by bank transfer. For a package holiday, check the company and the holiday on atol.org before you pay. If you think you've been caught, ring your bank straight away on the number on your card, or 159.
