Pulse

Once a week, what changed in AI that matters if you run a small business, or just use ChatGPT or Claude on your phone.

Each issue starts from RadarWindow's weekly brief, which tracks what's moving across the news, Reddit, Hacker News and GitHub. I pick the few things that would change how an ordinary person uses AI, and I explain them plainly. Every claim links to where it came from.

If nothing important changed for you that week, the issue will say so and stop.

The first issue goes up after the first weekly run. Until then there's nothing here, because I'd rather leave it empty than fill it with guesses.
