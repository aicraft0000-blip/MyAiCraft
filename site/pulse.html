Pulse

Once a week, what changed in AI that matters if you run a small business, or just use ChatGPT or Claude on your phone.

Each week I go looking: the AI companies' own announcements, the news, what people are arguing about. Then I pick what I found most worth your time. Usually that's something that changes how an ordinary person uses AI, or something to watch out for. Sometimes it'll simply be the thing I found most interesting, and I'll say why. Every claim links to where it came from.

If nothing important changed for you that week, the issue will say so and stop.

If something urgent comes up midweek, like a scam spreading or a privacy change that affects a lot of people, I'll post a short Heads-up here on a Thursday rather than make you wait until Monday.
