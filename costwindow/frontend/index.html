What CostWindow does

  • It tells you when money leaves that nothing explains. Log your real provider balance and CostWindow reconciles it against every tracked call. If spend left your account that no process accounts for, you get a tracking-gap alert — plus runway: how many days your balance lasts at current burn.
  • A kill switch that refuses the call. Route your calls through the CostWindow proxy and a killed process is refused before the provider is ever reached — a hard stop, not a polite request. Works on agents, cron jobs, and scripts you forgot were running.
  • Budgets that enforce themselves. Set a monthly cap per process. At 100%, the next call is blocked — no manual step, no morning-after surprise. Resets on the 1st.
  • One line to connect. The whole picture. Point your base URL at CostWindow — no SDK, no code changes, any language. Claude Code and Codex sessions track via a one-command hook. Flat subscriptions (Claude Pro, ChatGPT, tooling) — added once — sit in the same dashboard, so the number you see is your actual total AI spend.
